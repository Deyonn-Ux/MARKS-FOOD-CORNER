Refund and Return Policy

At Mark's Food Corner, customer satisfaction is our priority. If there is an issue with your order, we will do our best to resolve it promptly.

Eligible Refunds

Refunds or replacements may be provided if:

  • The wrong item was delivered
  • The food was damaged during delivery
  • The order was incomplete
  • The issue resulted from our error

Non-Eligible Refunds

Refunds will not be provided for:

  • Change of mind after order preparation
  • Delays caused by incorrect customer information
  • Delays caused by weather, traffic, or other circumstances beyond our control

Reporting an Issue

Customers must report concerns within 24 hours of receiving their order.

Please provide order details, photos if applicable, and a description of the issue.

Resolution

After reviewing the concern, we may offer a replacement item, store credit, or a partial or full refund, depending on the situation.

Contact Us

For assistance regarding refunds or returns, please contact our customer support team through our official communication channels.
