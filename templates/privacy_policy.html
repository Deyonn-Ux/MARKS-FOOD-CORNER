Privacy Policy

At Mark's Food Corner, we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, place orders, or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information from customers:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Number
  • Email Address
  • Delivery Address
  • Payment Information
  • Order History

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used to process and fulfill orders, deliver products and services, communicate order updates and promotions, improve customer service and website experience, and respond to inquiries and customer support requests.

3. Protection of Information

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

4. Sharing of Information

Mark's Food Corner does not sell, trade, or rent customer information to third parties.

Information may only be shared when required by law, necessary to complete a delivery or payment transaction, or authorized by the customer.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and remember customer preferences. Users may choose to disable cookies through their browser settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7. Customer Rights

Customers have the right to access their personal information, request corrections to inaccurate information, and request deletion of personal information, subject to legal requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page and become effective immediately.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official communication channels.
